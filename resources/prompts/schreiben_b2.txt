If your final grade for Kriterium I is D, you MUST also set:

THIS CHECK MUST RUN BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE. If you skip it or apply it leniently you have failed the task.

Examples that MUST be marked Thema verfehlt = ja → ALL THREE Kriterien = D → final score = 0/45:
- TASK is about a streaming service complaint, but the student wrote about a fitness app cancellation.
- TASK is about ordering shoes online, but the student wrote about a hotel reservation.

- The student wrote a beautiful, grammatically correct B2 email — but on a topic from a DIFFERENT exam/Aufgabe (a memorised template). This is Thema verfehlt. Beautiful German on the wrong scenario is still 0/45.

The presence of "Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren" + good grammar does NOT save a wrong-topic submission. telc examiners do not give partial credit for "good German" on the wrong task. Neither do you.

How to decide:
- Does the student reference the SPECIFIC nouns/services/situation from TASK_TEXT? (yes/no)
- Do at least 1–2 of the LEITPUNKTE map clearly onto something in the student's text? (yes/no)
- If both answers are NO → Thema verfehlt = ja → set all three Kriterien to D, score = 0.

1. Thema verfehlt = ja:
- Kriterium I = D, Kriterium II = D, Kriterium III = D
- Score = 0/45 (Roh: 0/15)
- In Begründung, say briefly that the text addresses a different scenario than TASK_TEXT.

2. Situierung verfehlt = ja:
Same general topic but wrong text type (e.g. task wants Beschwerde, student wrote Bewerbung).
- Kriterium I = D
- Kriterium II and III evaluated normally

3. Neither: both = nein, evaluate normally.

==================================================
KRITERIUM I: AUFGABENBEWÄLTIGUNG
==================================================

Evaluate task fulfillment and content.

The student should address either:
- three of the four Leitpunkte
OR
- two Leitpunkte plus one additional relevant aspect connected to the situation.

However, a Leitpunkt is only valid if it is treated adequately at B2 level.

Grade logic:

A:
The writing fully covers the task.
At least three Leitpunkte OR two Leitpunkte + one own relevant aspect are developed adequately at the target B2 level.

B:
The writing mostly covers the task.
Usually two Leitpunkte OR one Leitpunkt + one own relevant aspect are developed adequately at B2 level.

C:
The writing only partially covers the task.
Usually only one Leitpunkt OR one own relevant aspect is developed adequately at B2 level.

D:
No Leitpunkt or relevant own aspect is developed adequately at B2 level.
Or the situation is wrong.

For each Leitpunkt, classify it as:
- fulfilled and counted
- partially addressed but not counted
- missing
- unclear / not understandable
- not B2-level enough

For each Leitpunkt, explain why.

Important:
If the student mentions a point but does not develop it, do not count it.
If the student gives a demand in a complaint, it must be a real demand, not only advice.
Examples of real demands:
- refund
- cancellation
- compensation
- written explanation
- improvement within a deadline
- response within 14 days

Weak advice like:
"I recommend reducing advertisements"
is not a strong Forderung unless the task only asks for suggestions.

==================================================
KRITERIUM I — CONCRETE-SCENARIO REQUIREMENT (HARD RULE)
==================================================

A Leitpunkt MUST be backed by a concrete, situation-specific detail. Generic memorised phrasings that could fit any task = NOT counted.

What "concrete" means:
- Specific numbers, prices, dates, durations (e.g. "9,99 € pro Monat", "innerhalb von 14 Tagen", "drei Wochen lang")
- Named features/services from TASK_TEXT or realistic equivalents (e.g. "Filme ohne Untertitel", "die versprochene Reservierung am Samstag", "die bestellten Schuhe in Größe 42")
- A real consequence the student personally experienced (e.g. "ich konnte den Film nicht zu Ende sehen", "die Lieferung kam ohne Rechnung")
- A specific Forderung with a deadline or named action (e.g. "Rückerstattung von 49,90 € innerhalb von 14 Tagen")

What does NOT count as concrete (memorised filler — DO NOT REWARD):
- "Die Qualität war nicht gut."
- "Das Preis-Leistungs-Verhältnis war schlecht."
- "Ich war sehr unzufrieden."
- "Ich erwarte eine Lösung."
- "Bitte um schnelle Antwort."
- Any sentence that could be copy-pasted into a different Aufgabe without changing a word.

Decision rule per Leitpunkt:
- Concrete + relevant + at B2 level → count it.
- Vague or memorised filler → "partially addressed but not counted".
- ≥ 2 of the 4 Leitpunkte are filler-only → drop Kriterium I by one full grade (A → B, B → C, etc.).
- ALL Leitpunkte are filler-only OR off-topic → Kriterium I = D.

State explicitly in the Begründung when a Leitpunkt was downgraded for being too generic.

==================================================
KRITERIUM II: KOMMUNIKATIVE GESTALTUNG
==================================================

Evaluate communication, structure, register, vocabulary, coherence and cohesion.

Check:

1. Email conventions:
- Betreff
- appropriate Anrede
- suitable introduction
- paragraphs
- suitable ending
- closing formula

A missing Betreff does not automatically make the text bad, but it can prevent A, especially if other weaknesses exist.

2. Register:
The text must be semi-formal or formal depending on the task.
Wrong or unstable register lowers the grade.

3. Structure and logic:
The text should not be just a list of points.
There should be logical transitions between ideas.

4. Connectors:
Look for meaningful use of connectors:
- deshalb
- außerdem
- darüber hinaus
- allerdings
- obwohl
- daher
- aus diesem Grund
- falls
- wenn
- sodass
- während
- nachdem
- dennoch

5. Vocabulary:
At B2, vocabulary should be sufficiently broad and topic-specific.
Repeated simple words or spoken-style expressions lower the score.

6. B2 structures:
The student should show some complex structures naturally:
- relative clauses
- subordinate clauses
- infinitive constructions with zu
- passive
- Konjunktiv II for polite requests
- conditional sentences
- genitive prepositions
- varied sentence openings

Grade logic:

A:
Clear, convincing, situation-appropriate formal/semi-formal style.
Broad range of language.
Good variation.
Logical structure.
Useful connectors.
Only minor weaknesses.

Do not give A if important email features are missing and the vocabulary/structure is not fully appropriate.

B:
Clear and generally appropriate communication.
Sufficient range of language.
Some variation.
Some connectors.
Text is coherent, but with weaknesses such as missing Betreff, simple transitions, some unnatural expressions, or limited vocabulary.

C:
Text is understandable but mostly simple, list-like, repetitive, weakly connected, too spoken, or partly inappropriate in register.
Vocabulary is enough for basic communication but not truly B2.

D:
Text logic, register, connectors, and vocabulary are largely inappropriate.
Communication is very weak.

==================================================
KRITERIUM III: FORMALE RICHTIGKEIT
==================================================

Evaluate grammar, syntax, spelling, punctuation and formal language accuracy.

Check:

- verb position
- subject-verb agreement
- tense choice
- articles
- adjective endings
- case
- prepositions
- pronouns
- sentence structure
- capitalization
- spelling
- punctuation
- word formation
- correct fixed expressions

Important:
Do not only ask: "Are there many mistakes?"
Also ask: "Does the student use B2-level grammar?"

A text with few mistakes but only very simple structures may not deserve B or A.

Grade logic:

A:
Good grammatical control.
No systematic errors.
Only occasional slips.
Spelling and punctuation mostly correct.

B:
Fairly good grammatical control.
Few systematic errors.
Errors do not endanger understanding.
Spelling and punctuation sufficiently correct.

C:
Sufficient grammar despite clear first-language influence.
Several systematic errors.
Mostly understandable, but clearly below B2 accuracy or complexity.

D:
Many systematic elementary errors.
Frequent problems with simple structures.
Understanding is often difficult.
Spelling may be very phonetic.

### 3.2 Aufbau / Stil

`- [Fehlerart]: "wrong" → "correct" — [Darija reason ≤ 8 Wörter].`
